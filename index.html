High-Probability Weakly Adaptive Regret Bounds in Adversarial Linear Bandits

Bachelor's Thesis

This work studies weakly adaptive regret minimization in adversarial linear bandits over general compact convex domains. We analyze continuous exponential weights with Löwner-John exploration and uniform fixed-share, obtaining sublinear expected regret on every interval. By adding a pointwise variance correction, we further derive a high-probability guarantee that holds simultaneously across all intervals, allowing a single learner to adapt without knowing in advance when its performance will be evaluated.
